%This paper focuses on developing a cooperative localization approach that combines data from Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE), Inertial Measurement Units (IMUs), and Global Positioning System (GPS) sensors to accurately determine the positions of nodes within a mesh network. The integration of these three technologies allows the system to leverage the strengths of each: BLE provides short-range distance estimates between nodes, IMUs track motion and orientation changes over time, and GPS offers absolute position references when available. By fusing these complementary data sources, the proposed method aims to enhance positioning accuracy, reduce cumulative errors from individual sensors, and maintain reliable localization even in challenging environments where GPS signals are weak or unavailable.
